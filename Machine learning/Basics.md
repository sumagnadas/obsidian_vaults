## Definitions
---
- **Statistical Learning** - In Machine Learning, we make a model using an observed response $Y$ based on predictors $X_1 , X_2 ,..., X_N$. We try to estimate a relationship between $Y$ and the predictors $X$ and the fundamental model is,
  $$Y=f(X)+\epsilon$$ Where $\epsilon$ is random error.
- Estimation of $f (\hat{f})$ helps us predict future outcomes or understand the relation between the predictors. 
- $argmin_xf(x)$ and $argmax_xf(x)$ are two functions which return the point/value x where the function $f$ reaches its minimum or maximum respectively.
